          What Phish Detector checks

          Phish Detector looks for signals commonly associated with phishing and online scams:

          • Website impersonation — does the URL imitate a legitimate company, bank, or service?
          • Lookalike domains — does it use character substitutions or deceptive spelling?
          • Suspicious language — does the message create urgency, fear, or pressure?
          • Sensitive information requests — does it ask for your BVN, OTP, PIN, password, or card details?
          • Threat intelligence — does the URL appear in known phishing, malware, or scam databases?
          • Redirects and shortened links — where possible, does the link hide or redirect to a suspicious destination?

          Clear results. Practical guidance.

          If Phish Detector identifies suspicious activity, you'll get a clear explanation of the signals it found and guidance on what to do next.

          If no obvious threat is detected, you'll still get the checks that were performed so you can make an informed decision.

          Privacy-first: Messages are analyzed on your device. Links may be checked against public threat-intelligence services. Nothing you paste is stored on our servers.
